# Crystalline Media Website

A responsive starter website for Crystalline Media.

## Files
- index.html — complete website
- style.css — design and responsive layout

## Before publishing
1. Replace the placeholder CM logo with your official Crystalline Media logo.
2. Add your official phone number and email in the Contact section.
3. Replace the sample stories with real articles.
4. Add your Facebook/YouTube video URLs.
5. Connect the contact form to an email/form service or a backend.
6. Purchase a domain such as your preferred Crystalline Media web address and hosting.
